Astro Modular in under a minute. Here's what the video covers.

- **Your Obsidian vault, published.** Write in Obsidian with Vault CMS, and every note in your vault's `posts` folder becomes a post.
- **Turn on what you need.** The command palette, table of contents, reading time, word count, tags, and graph view each switch on or off in `src/config.ts`.
- **17 color themes.** Each one in light and dark, or make your own in `src/themes/custom/`.
- **Wikilinks, callouts, and embeds.** Obsidian-native content renders on the site the way it looks in your vault, Bases included.
- **See how it all connects.** The graph view maps every post and the links between them.
- **Free and open source.** MIT licensed and [on GitHub](https://github.com/davidvkimball/astro-modular).
